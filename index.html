I am a Ph.D. student at Zhejiang University, having joined in 2023, advised by Hehe Fan. My research focuses on structured reasoning, autonomous research agents, and multi-agent collaboration.

I study how language models can organize their reasoning, how agents can investigate difficult questions using traceable evidence, and how multiple agents can coordinate complex tasks. My public work includes Structured Reasoning, Super Research, and VillagerAgent.

From September 2025 to October 2026, I have a research collaboration with Ant Group. I also serve as a conference reviewer for ACL 2026 and ICLR 2027.
